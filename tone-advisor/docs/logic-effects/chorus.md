# Chorus — Logic Pro

Source: logic-pro-mac-effects-user-guide.txt, lines 14269–14301

Add via: Modulation > Chorus in a channel strip Audio Effect plug-in menu.

---

## Overview

Delays the original signal, and the delay time is modulated with an LFO. The delayed, modulated signal is mixed with the original dry signal. Creates the impression of multiple instruments or voices playing in unison. Adds fullness, richness, and movement to low or sustained sounds.

---

## Parameters

- **Rate knob and field**: Frequency (speed) of the LFO
- **Intensity knob and field**: Modulation amount
- **D-Mode button**: Turn on to introduce a spatial filtering effect that resembles a well-known vintage processor
- **Mix knob and field**: Balance between dry and wet signals
