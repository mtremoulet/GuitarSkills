# Echo — Logic Pro

Source: logic-pro-mac-effects-user-guide.txt, lines 4133–4194

Add via: Delay > Echo in a channel strip Audio Effect plug-in menu.

---

## Overview

Simple echo effect that always synchronizes delay time to project tempo. Quick tempo-locked echo effects.

---

## Parameters

- **Note pop-up menu and knob**: Grid resolution of delay time in musical note durations, based on project tempo. Notes (and dots) displayed around the Note knob. Click buttons/dots or rotate knob to choose exact sync value.
- **÷2 and ×2 buttons**: Halve or double the current delay time
- **Feedback knob and field**: How often the delay effect is repeated
- **Color slider and field**: Harmonic content (tone/color) of the delay signal
- **Dry slider and field**: Amount of original signal
- **Wet slider and field**: Amount of effect signal
