# Crunck V2 Guitar Amplifier — Nembrini Audio (Free)

Source: https://www.nembriniaudio.com/products/crunck-v2-guitar-amplifier

---

## Overview

Original Nembrini Audio design (freeware) — not modeled on a specific real-world amplifier. Single-channel, high-headroom design with a very wide gain range. The name "Crunck" reflects its crunch-focused character.

---

## Controls

- **Gain**: Wide range — low settings produce warm clean-to-blues tones; high settings deliver modern rock and metal saturation
- **Bass / Mid / Treble**: Three-band passive tone stack
- **Presence**: High-frequency power amp shimmer
- **Master**: Output level

---

## Cabinet

Built-in 4x12 cabinet loaded with Vintage 30 speakers (bypassable). When bypassed, output can feed a separate IR loader (e.g., Torpedo Wall of Sound or any convolution plugin).

---

## Tone Notes

- Wide gain range means it can cover a surprising amount of ground — from warm edge-of-breakup blues (Gain 2–3) to compressed modern rock (Gain 7–8).
- As a free plugin, it's a solid first-in-chain amp when you want British-leaning saturation without opening a heavier plugin.
- Works well as a "utility crunch amp" for rhythm tracks where you don't need the specificity of the JCM800 or Divided 11.
- Bypass the built-in cab and use a third-party IR for the best results — the built-in V30 IR is serviceable but generic.
