# Nembrini Jazz Chorus Solid State — Documentation

The Nembrini Jazz Chorus Solid State is an emulation of the iconic Roland JC-120 amplifier. It is known for its pristine solid-state clean tones and its unique "Space Chorus" effect.

## Signal Chain Sections
The interface is divided into four main functional sections:
1. **Stomps**: Pre-amp effects (Noise Gate, Compressor, Auto Swell, Fuzz).
2. **Amplifier**: The main controls for preamp, tone, and built-in effects.
3. **Cabinet**: Speaker simulations and microphone placement.
4. **Post Effects**: Digital studio-grade effects (EQ, Delay, Studio Compressor).

---

## 1. Amplifier Controls
Replicates the front panel of the original hardware.

- **Bright Switch**: Adds high-end clarity and sparkle.
- **Volume**: Controls the input level/gain of the preamp stage.
- **Treble / Middle / Bass**: Standard 3-band EQ.
- **Distortion**: Controls the amount of solid-state saturation.
- **Reverb**: Controls the built-in spring reverb level.
- **Mode Switch**: Toggles between **CHORUS**, **OFF**, and **VIBRATO**.
- **Speed**: Adjusts vibrato rate (active in Vibrato mode only).
- **Depth**: Adjusts vibrato intensity (active in Vibrato mode only).
- *Note: Chorus is a fixed "Space Chorus" setting.*

---

## 2. Pre-Amplifier Stomps
- **Noise Gate**: Threshold and Decay.
- **Compressor**: Ratio and Threshold.
- **Auto Swell**: Speed and Depth for volume swells.
- **Fuzz**: Drive, Level, Filter.

---

## 3. Cabinet & IR Loader
- **6 Cabinet Models**: Including the RLD 2x12 JC120.
- **4 Microphone Models**: Dynamic 57, Ribbon 121, Condenser 414, Dynamic 421.
- **Mic Placement**: Position (distance from center) and Distance (distance from speaker).
- **Impulse Loader**: Load up to 3 custom IRs.

---

## 4. Post-Amplifier Effects
- **Equalizer**: Digital 4-band EQ.
- **Delay**: Time, Feedback, Shimmer.
- **Studio Compressor**: Final transparent gain reduction.

---

## 5. Gain Staging (Nembrini Standard)
- **Input Slider**: Transparent trim at the plugin boundary. Use this to ensure the model isn't clipping at the input.
- **Output Slider**: Transparent trim after the cabinet simulation. Use this to target final levels (-12 to -10 dBFS).
