# MRH810 V2 Lead Series Guitar Amplifier — Nembrini Audio

Source: https://nembriniaudiodownload.blob.core.windows.net/installer/Mrh810V2/Manual.pdf

---

## Overview

Emulation of a Marshall JCM800 2210 — the two-channel 100W head that defined classic rock and metal tone from the early 1980s onward. Faithful reproduction of the British high-gain character that defined an era.

---

## Front Panel Controls

### Power
Bypass the plugin — compare processed vs. unprocessed signal.

### Master Section (power amp stage)

| Control | Function |
|---------|---------|
| Presence | Adjusts high-frequency content — affects power amp stage brightness and bite |
| Volume | Master volume of the amplifier (power amp output level) |
| Reverb | Amount of reverb added to the signal |

*Note: The MRH810 V2 has a built-in Reverb control in the master section — it is separate from any Logic or bus reverb.*

### Channel Selector
Switches between Lead Channel and Clean Channel.

### Lead Channel

| Control | Function |
|---------|---------|
| Gain | Amount of distortion in the preamp section — the primary tonal character control |
| Volume | Volume of the Lead Channel preamp stage before the master section |
| Bass | Low-frequency tone stack |
| Middle | Mid-frequency tone stack |
| Treble | High-frequency tone stack |
| LED | Illuminated when Lead Channel is active |

### Clean Channel

| Control | Function |
|---------|---------|
| Volume | Volume of the Clean Channel preamp stage before the master section |
| Bass | Low-frequency tone |
| Treble | High-frequency tone |
| LED | Illuminated when Clean Channel is active |

*Note: The Clean Channel has a 2-band tone stack (Bass/Treble only); Lead Channel has a full 3-band stack (Bass/Middle/Treble).*

## Cabinet — Impulse Loader Panel

Access by clicking the Cabinet/Impulse Loader button.

### Recording Chain Mode (3 buttons)
Selects operating mode. Cleaner, Noise Gate, and Levels remain active in all three modes.

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Cabinet | Uses built-in cabinet/recording chain emulations |
| Impulse Loader | Uses loaded third-party IR files |
| Bypass | Bypasses entire cab/recording chain — use with external IR loader or real power amp/speakers |

---

### Cabinet Mode

**6 Built-in Cabinet Emulations:**

| Name | Based On |
|------|----------|
| MRH 4x12 T75 | Marshall 1960B 4x12 with Celestion G12T-75W speakers |
| MRH 4x12 G12 | Marshall 1960B 4x12 with Celestion G12T-25W speakers |
| MB RECT 4x12 V30 | Mesa Rectifier 4x12 with Celestion Vintage 30 speakers |
| ORANGE 4x12 V30 | Orange 4x12 with Celestion Vintage 30 speakers |
| BG 2x12 V30 | Bogner 2x12 with Celestion Vintage 30 speakers |
| TWEED 4x10 P10Q | Fender Tweed Bassman 4x10 with Jensen P10Q speakers |

**4 Microphone Emulations (2 mics available simultaneously):**

| Name | Based On |
|------|----------|
| Dynamic 57 | Shure SM-57 dynamic microphone |
| Ribbon 121 | Royer Labs R-121 ribbon microphone |
| Condenser 414 | AKG C414 condenser microphone |
| Dynamic 421 | Sennheiser MD-421 dynamic microphone |

Each mic: On-Axis/Off-Axis switch, Position knob, Distance knob.

**Mixer (3 faders):**
- Fader 1: Mic 1 volume
- Fader 2: Mic 2 volume
- Fader 3: Stereo ambience microphones
- Each channel: Pan, Solo, Mute, Phase inversion switch. Pan active only in stereo and mono-to-stereo instances.

---

### Impulse Loader Mode

Load up to 3 third-party IR files simultaneously. Per IR slot: Empty, Browse, Load, Volume, Pan, Phase, Solo, Mute.

---

### Cleaner Section (always active, all modes)

Two named filter toggles — on/off only, not parameterized:

| Filter | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Rumbling | Reduces low-frequency content |
| Harsh | Reduces high-frequency content |

### Noise Gate (always active)

| Control | Function |
|---------|---------|
| Power | Activates the noise gate |
| Threshold | Level at which gate activates |
| Range | Amount of noise reduction |

### Levels (always active)

| Control | Range | Function |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Input | −∞ to +12 dB | Signal level entering the plugin |
| Output | −∞ to +12 dB | Signal level exiting the plugin |
| Fix button | — | Keeps input level constant when switching presets |

## Tone Character Notes

- The JCM800 2210 is the defining sound of early 1980s British rock and metal — AC/DC, Guns N' Roses, Metallica (early), countless others.
- Lead Channel is the signature voice. Not subtle — high-gain, compressed, snarling. Gain knob is the most critical control.
- Clean Channel responds exceptionally well to pedals in front (808, Clon Minotaur, or any transparent overdrive) — excellent pedal platform.
- Middle control on the Lead Channel is the most interactive of the three tone controls — British amps are characteristically mid-forward.
- **MRH 4x12 T75** is the canonical Marshall 4x12 sound — the reference cab for this amp.
- **TWEED 4x10 P10Q** is an interesting option for a warmer, less aggressive British character — Bassman-adjacent texture with the JCM800 gain structure.
- Built-in Reverb in the Master section: use sparingly in conjunction with a Logic reverb bus, or use it instead of Logic reverb for a self-contained tone.
