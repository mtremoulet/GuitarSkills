## Overview

End-to-end virtual studio for guitar sounds. Contains 11 amp emulations, 35 cab/mic combinations, and onboard stompbox + studio effects (up to 5 Pre FX + 5 Post FX). Self-contained signal chain in a single plugin.

**Important**: The Enigmatic '82 amp (and Lion '68) are accessible WITHIN Paradise Guitar Studio — they are not standalone plugins in the inventory. To use the Enigmatic '82, open Paradise Guitar Studio.

---

## Architecture

Signal path: Pre FX (up to 5 effects) → Amp + Cab & Mics → Room → Post FX (up to 5 effects) → Noise Gate → Output Limiter

Three main sections (top navigation bar):
1. Pre FX — effects before amp
2. Amp and Cab & Mics — core tone
3. Post FX — effects after amp

---

## Input Level

Calibrated for Hi-Z instrument inputs (same as other UAD amp plugins). Set audio interface preamp gain to minimum. Reduce IN knob for preamplified or hot signals. Increase IN knob for low-level inputs.

---

## Amps

11 amps total. Lion '68 and Enigmatic '82 have multiple models (selectable in gallery right panel).

Notable amp: **Enigmatic '82** — ODS-style boutique amp. Voices: Suede (warm, round), Silver (bright, articulate), Cream (rich, mid-heavy), Black (aggressive, tighter). Controls: Drive, Bass, Mid, Treble, Presence, Master, Input Mode. Very dynamic — cleans up with guitar volume roll-off. Excellent for vocal, emotional overdrive and blues lead.

## Built-in Effects (Pre FX and Post FX)

### Stompboxes (Pre FX)
- TS Overdrive — classic green overdrive; adds midrange and cuts low end
- Nashville OD — warm transparent tube-like distortion; "Nashville favorite," pairs well with Dream/Showtime amps
- Metal Box — high-gain distortion; Filter knob cuts highs clockwise
- Gold Overdrive — "classic Gold Overdrive lead sound"
- Uni-Vibe (Psyche-Vibe) — "soaring psychedelic blues tones" with Lion Bass model
- Brigade Chorus — warbling, chewy gain before amp
- Multi-tap chorus

### Studio Effects (Post FX)
- 1176 Compression
- Plate and spring reverbs
- Tape delay
- EQ
- Amp-style tube tremolo
- Multi-tap six-voice chorus
- Others

## Notes on Using Paradise Guitar Studio vs. Standalone Plugins

Paradise Guitar Studio is self-contained — its amps, cabs, and internal effects all live within the one plugin. When building a signal chain using external plugins (e.g., standalone 1176, standalone Capitol Chambers), use the standalone amp plugins (Lion '68, Dream '65, Woodrow '55, Ruby '63, Showtime '64) instead.

Use Paradise Guitar Studio when you want all-in-one convenience, built-in stompboxes, or specifically need the Enigmatic '82 or additional amp models not available standalone.
