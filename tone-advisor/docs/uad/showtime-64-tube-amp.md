## Overview

Based on a powerful, high-headroom tube amp first introduced in the early 1960s (Fender Showman/Twin family). Offered as a head and 2x12 speaker cabinet. Designed for large venues with minimal breakup. Famous users: Dick Dale, The Beatles, David Gilmour, Keith Richards, John Frusciante.

**Character**: Immense clean headroom even at high volumes. Huge low end response with enough treble for classic American tube amp twang. Beefy transformers and power tubes contribute to a muscular clean tone. Neutral amp character and high headroom make it an excellent pedal platform.

## Controls

### In
Adjusts input level to the plug-in. Hover to see HI-Z and LINE labels.
- Default center position = HI-Z (calibrated for Apollo/Volt Hi-Z input with minimum preamp gain)
- Reduce toward LINE for hotter signals (pre-amplified tracks, humbuckers, active pickups)
- IN value does not change when loading presets

**Input guidelines**: Connect to Hi-Z instrument input, set hardware preamp gain to minimum, set IN to default center (HI-Z).

### Bright / Normal
- **Bright**: High end boost added to amplifier sound. Most noticeable at lower Volume settings; effect decreases as Volume increases (matches original bright cap behavior).
- **Normal**: Reduces brightness. Warmer character.
- Bright is off by default.

### Volume
Adjusts amp gain. As Volume increases, character becomes more overdriven and compressed. Minimum level = lowest usable volume on original hardware. Output volume is automatically adjusted as Volume is turned up.

**Note**: This is the gain/volume control on the original amp. For clean post-amp volume adjustments, use OUT.

### Treble
Adjusts high frequency response of the amp's tone stack.

### Middle
Adjusts midrange frequency response of the amp's tone stack.

### Bass
Adjusts low frequency response of the amp's tone stack.

### Room
Adjusts the amount of room tone mixed into the signal.
- Noon = good starting point
- Fully off = isolation booth character
- Turning up adds ambience from room microphones
- There is no fully wet Room sound

### Vibrato (Speed / Intensity)
Complex harmonic vibrato circuit (pitch-based, not amplitude tremolo) — closer to a rotary speaker than standard tremolo.
- Enable/disable via LED button between Speed and Intensity knobs
- **Speed**: Vibrato rate
- **Intensity**: Vibrato depth

### Out
Adjusts output level from the plug-in. Default = roughly same level as bypassed signal.

## Cabinet

The plug-in includes the original amp's paired 2x12 speaker cabinet. No alternative cab selections — this is the only cab option.

---

## Microphones

Three microphone setups. Select from dropdown or previous/next arrows.

| Mic | Character |
|-----|-----------|
| 57 + Ribbon 121 | Dynamic 57 blended with ribbon 121. 57 = aggressive upper midrange punch and presence. 121 = rich, smooth low end, reduced top end. Classic guitar cabinet combination. |
| Condenser 414 | Detailed and transparent. Extended low and high frequency response. Full-bodied representation of the amp. |
| Ribbon 160 | Warm and natural. Smooth top-end that tames higher frequencies. Especially smooth when adding distortion/overdrive. Double-ribbon, hypercardioid ribbon mic with focused detail and warmth. |

## Tone-Targeted Starting Points

*Jazz / warm clean:* Normal, Volume 3, Treble 4, Middle 5, Bass 6, Room 2–3, Mic: Ribbon 160.

*Clean pedal platform (neutral):* Normal, Volume 3–4, Treble 5, Middle 5, Bass 5, Room 3, Mic: 57 + Ribbon 121.

*Detailed full-range clean:* Normal, Volume 4, Treble 5, Middle 5, Bass 5, Room 3, Mic: Condenser 414.

*Bright sparkle (lower volume):* Bright, Volume 3, Treble 6, Middle 5, Bass 4, Room 3, Mic: Condenser 414.
