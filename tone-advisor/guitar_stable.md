# Guitar Stable Reference

## Electric Guitars

### 1. 2024 Fender Player II Telecaster
* **Finish:** British Racing Green
* **Hardware:** Rosewood fingerboard, six-saddle modern bridge
* **Strings:** Thomastik-Infeld Jazz Swing Flats (10s)
* **Notes:** The "Home Base." Warm, comfortable, and articulate.

### 2. 2014 Gibson Les Paul Studio
* **Finish:** Ebony (Serial: 140020207)
* **Specs:** 120th Anniversary Edition, 490R/490T Alnico II Pickups
* **Strings:** D'Addario XS (10s)
* **Notes:** The ultimate "Smooth Refinement" and sustain machine. 

### 3. Mid-1980s Squier Stratocaster "Partscaster"
* **Specs:** Made in Japan SQ-series body / E-series neck, upgraded with Fender Tex-Mex pre-wired pickguard (tortoiseshell)
* **Finish:** Light Blue with Tortoiseshell Pickguard
* **Strings:** Stringjoy Broadways (10s)
* **Notes:** The "Funky Quack." Hot, punchy, pure nickel vintage warmth.

### 4. 2013 Epiphone Sheraton II
* **Finish:** Natural
* **Strings:** Thomastik-Infeld Jazz Swing Flats (11s)
* **Notes:** The "Velvet Jazz Box." Heavier strings for maximum acoustic resonance and a traditional jazz feel.

### 5. 2008 Epiphone Les Paul Standard Plus Top
* **Finish:** Vintage Sunburst
* **Strings:** D'Addario XS (10-46)
* **Notes:** The bright, sustaining classic rock workhorse.

### 6. 2023 Revelation RFT DLX
* **Specs:** Thinline Tele Style, Alan Entwistle H90 Pickups
* **Strings:** D'Addario XS (10s)
* **Notes:** The "Aggressive Growler." Bright, coated rounds to accentuate the P90-style bite.
