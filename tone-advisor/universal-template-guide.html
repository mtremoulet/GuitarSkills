Hardware Baseline
1. Physical Front-End Configuration
To ensure that the dynamic compilers yield consistent volume, compression threshold, and EQ colors in Logic, your physical guitar front-end must be locked to a transparent baseline.
Philosophy
We want the physical preamp to do as little coloring as possible unless it is explicitly requested by a toneprint. Setting a quiet volume and bypassing speaker cabinet IRs ensures the dry signal reaches the interface at a high-fidelity level, ready for DAW processing.
✔ Transparency Floor Calibration Checklist
Hardware Control Target Setting Purpose
TONEX One BYPASS (Stomp Mode Off) Keeps the guitar signal completely dry and transparent.
Tone King Channel Rhythm Cleanest channel with maximum headroom.
Tone King Volume 2.0 to 3.0 Keeps the preamp in the "transparency zone," avoiding early tube breakup.
Tone King Attenuation 5.0 Moderate/unity level going into the interface.
Tone King Bass / Treble 5.0 (Noon / Flat) Gives a flat response, leaving EQ shaping entirely to the plugin stack.
Tone King IR BYPASS Crucial: avoids stacking multiple cabinet emulations with Logic.
Audient iD14 Preamp Set Gain for peaks around −18 dBFS Protects head-room and matches gain staging expectations.

Operating Guide
The 3-Step Operation Workflow
When loading a new toneprint (e.g., Wes Clean, Velvet Crunch, etc.), this baseline structure lets you get your sound fully set up and dialed in under 15 seconds.
▶ How to load Tones in Seconds
1
De-Bypass the Target Signal Chain:
Look at the toneprint file. Turn on (de-bypass) only the specific plugins mentioned in the signal chain. Keep all other slots in their bypassed state to save CPU and headroom.
2
Load the prefix-grouped User Presets:
Click the preset drop-down menu in each activated plugin window. Find the user directory and select the matching Toneprint - [Tone Name] preset. (e.g., choose Toneprint - Sheraton Jazz Acoustic in UA Paradise, Channel EQ, LA-2A, and Capitol Chambers).
3
Calibrate the Fader Return Blends:
Align your faders to match the toneprint's fader balances. For example, slide the "Acoustic" Aux strip to −8 dB and the "Reverb Return" Aux to −12 dB.
Physical Touch Tip: Roll your physical guitar tone knob to 7 and the volume knob to **8** to complete the touch-sensitive vintage analog environment!
