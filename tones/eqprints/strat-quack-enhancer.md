# Glassy Strat Quack

The magic of a Stratocaster lies in its out-of-phase pickup combinations (Positions 2 and 4). These positions feature a natural phase cancellation that hollows out the midrange and accentuates the high-frequency "glass." 

This EQprint enhances this natural hollow character by scooping the boxy midrange and boosting the glassy presence bands, making your funk rhythm, neo-soul lines, and clean chord progressions pop with chime.

## Why this curve works (Band-by-Band)

- **31.25 Hz & 62.5 Hz (Cut to -12dB and -8dB)**: Standard high-pass filtering to keep the low end tight and punchy.
- **125 Hz & 250 Hz (Cut to -2dB and -1dB)**: Slight cut to prevent any low-end muddiness, keeping the rhythm strumming crisp.
- **500 Hz (Cut to -4dB)**: Scoops out the boxy midrange. By cutting here, we amplify the "hollowed-out" phase effect that defines the Strat quack.
- **1 kHz (0dB)**: Left neutral to preserve the core note articulation.
- **2 kHz & 4 kHz (Boost to +3dB and +4dB)**: This is the chime engine. Boosting here highlights the glassy transients and high-mid snap of the strings, providing the crisp "bell-like" tone.
- **8 kHz (Boost to +2dB)**: Adds extra shimmer and sparkle to the pick attack.
- **16 kHz (Cut to -4dB)**: Rolled off slightly to prevent digital hiss and keep the sparkle musical rather than sterile.
