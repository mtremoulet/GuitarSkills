# Bill Withers — 70s Soul & Funk Rhythm

## Target Sound

This toneprint captures the warm, syncopated, and deeply expressive rhythm guitar style foundational to Bill Withers' legendary 1970s recordings like **"Use Me"** (played by Ray Jackson) and **"Lean On Me."**

70s soul rhythm guitar relies on clean, woody, edge-of-breakup warmth with exceptional low-mid punch. Notes must sound clear and articulate during complex chord vamps and muted funk scratching without sounding harsh or overly bright.

Using the **UADx Woodrow '55** (Fender Tweed Deluxe emulation) set to a warm, high-headroom sweet spot, paired with LA-2A optical dynamic smoothing and tight chamber reverb, this chain glues your humbucker rhythm playing into the groove.

## Starting Point Guide

- **Pickup Selection & Controls:** Switch to your **Epiphone Sheraton II** or **Les Paul Studio** neck pickup (or neck + bridge blend). Set the guitar volume to **8** and guitar tone to **7** for that iconic muted 70s soul warmth.
- **Rhythmic Technique:** Perfect for muted funk scratches and syncopated chord stabs (like the iconic riff in "Use Me"). Keep your picking hand relaxed to allow the natural tube sag and optical compression to bounce with the tempo.
