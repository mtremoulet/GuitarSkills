# Paradise Pedal Platform (Single-Coil Variant)

## Target Sound
Optimized for the chime and lower output of single-coils. The **Enigmatic '82** is set to provide more "body" in the low-mids to ensure the Tele or Strat doesn't sound too thin or "ice-picky" when pedals are engaged.

## Signal Chain

### 1. Amp: Enigmatic '82 — The Foundation

| Control | Setting | Purpose |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Voice | Silver | High-headroom, bright but full |
| Drive | 3.5 | Slightly higher to compensate for lower SC output |
| Bass | 5.0 | Fuller low end for single-coil body |
| Mid | 5.0 | Neutral |
| Treble | 5.5 | Slightly rounded to avoid harshness with bridge pickups |
| Presence | 5.0 | Balanced |
| Master | 7.5 | Pushed slightly more for power |

**Pre-FX / Pre-Amp Stompbox Option**

| Control | Setting | Purpose |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Pedal | **Gold Overdrive** | Transparent clean boost on hand |
| State | **Disabled** (Off) | Bypass by default; engage for clean solo boost or pushing front end |
| Gain | **0.0** | Zero added distortion; pure clean boost |
| Output | **7.5** | Pushes front end of amp for singing sustain and level lift |
| Treble | **4.5** | Slightly rounded high end for smooth boost response |

### 2. Cabinet & Room — Space
| Component | Setting | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Cabinet | 1x12 EV12 | Flat, wide-range response |
| Room | Studio A | Small, controlled space |
| Room Mix | 18% | Slightly more mix for single-coil "bloom" |
